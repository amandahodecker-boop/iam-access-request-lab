# Project Scenario

## Fictional environment

NovaCore Technologies is a fictional mid-sized technology company used exclusively for this IAM laboratory.

The company has multiple business areas and corporate systems that require controlled access based on business needs, job responsibilities, and approval workflows.

> All names, users, systems, roles, departments, and business scenarios in this project are fictional. No internal or confidential information from real organizations is used.
## Business areas

NovaCore Technologies currently uses the following business areas in this lab:

- Finance
- Human Resources
- Operations
- Technology
- Information Security

## Corporate systems

### NovaERP

Corporate system used for administrative, financial, and inventory-related activities.

### PeopleHub

Human Resources system used to manage employee-related information.

### ServiceFlow

Service management system used to register and track internal requests.
